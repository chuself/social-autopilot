Privacy Policy

Operra · Last updated 21 August 2026

This policy explains how Operra (“we”, “us”) handles information in connection with operra.tech and the software we operate to manage our own social media presence on Facebook and Instagram.

Who we are

Operra is a hotel and restaurant management system operating in Tanzania. Contact: adminoperra@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us directly — if you message us on WhatsApp, email, or comment on our posts, we receive the content of that message and your public profile name.
  • Publicly available engagement data — aggregate counts of likes, comments, shares and reach on our own posts, provided by Meta.
  • Website usage — standard server logs when you visit operra.tech.

We do not collect, buy, or scrape personal data about people who have not contacted us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your questions about Operra.
  • To understand which of our posts are useful, in aggregate, so we publish better ones.
  • To operate and improve our own social media accounts.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data for advertising targeting.

Automated replies

Some replies to public comments on our Facebook and Instagram posts are drafted automatically. Messages that appear to need a person are passed to a human instead of being answered automatically. You can always reach a person at the contact address above.

Facebook and Instagram

We use Meta’s official APIs to publish to, and read engagement on, accounts we own. We do not access your personal Facebook or Instagram account, your friends list, your private messages, or any data belonging to accounts we do not control. Your use of Facebook and Instagram is also governed by Meta’s own privacy policy.

Sharing

We share information only with the service providers needed to operate the above — principally Meta (Facebook, Instagram), Google (hosting and language services), and GitHub (hosting). We disclose information if required by law.

Retention

We keep enquiry messages for as long as needed to answer them and to keep a record of our conversation with you. Aggregate post performance data is kept while the account operates.

Your rights

You may ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to adminoperra@gmail.com and we will respond.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any information we hold about you, email adminoperra@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion”. We will confirm once it is done.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page.
