ANTI-THEFT AUDIT

“Lost amenities aren't "shrinkage" — they're an unmeasured ledger. Measure it, and shrinkage becomes a number you can fix.”
Tablet & phone first

Built for the supervisor's tablet at the station and the maid's phone in the corridor — not a desktop reporting tool.

Anti-theft from day one

Per-item amenity ledger is the default — water bottles, slippers, minibar — every used item logged.

Knock-first protocol

Respects guest DND by default. Missed knocks queue a supervisor follow-up instead of forcing entry.

Offline-safe sign-off

Sign-off and inspection results queue locally and re-sequence cleanly when the connection returns.
