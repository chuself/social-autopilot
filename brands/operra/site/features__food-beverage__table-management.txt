RUSH MODE

The two-tap path to seated.

When the dining room fills up, every extra tap costs you covers. Rush Mode collapses the workflow to a single visible breadcrumb — Outlet › Table › Action — with three giant action cards.

Open POS — walk-in, jumps straight to the menu with the table attached.
Seat reservation — picks from today's bookings, party size pre-filled.
Hold table — mark reserved with no order yet, no awkward fake tab.
Median 4.2 seconds from "guest arrives" to "table open."

TWO MODES, ONE FLOOR

Manage when it's calm. Rush when it isn't.
Manage mode
CALM

Plan zones, edit layouts, assign waiters, reorder tables, set reservations — full floor controls visible, KPIs across the top.

Rush mode
FULL HOUSE

Chrome collapses, hit-targets grow. Outlet › Table › Action — two taps to seat, two to settle.

Move a tab
MID-SERVICE

A couple wants the patio? Transfer the tab — order history, waiter and folio all follow.

Split & settle
ON CLOSE

Per-guest or per-item splits; cash, card, mobile money or the room — combinations welcome.

Hold the table
RESERVATIONS

Reserved tables surface on the map at the right time, so the host never double-seats.

PUBLIC RESERVATIONS

A booking link that doesn't leak your floor.

Share one link from Instagram, WhatsApp or your website. Guests pick party size, outlet and time. The reservation lands on your floor map — you confirm or decline from the same screen the host uses.

Branded with your restaurant name — no Operra branding on the public form.
Guests never see your live table availability or floor layout.
Special requests (allergies, occasions) sit next to the booking, not in a separate note.
Requests are confirmable — you control the floor; guests never auto-book.
