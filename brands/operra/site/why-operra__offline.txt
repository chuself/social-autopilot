THE STORY

When the power dropped at 11:42 PM, check-in didn't.

A 22-room boutique on the coast loses fibre for the third time this week. The generator hums, the lights stay on, but the international PMS the previous owner installed shows a single spinning circle. Guests are queuing. The receptionist starts writing on a paper folio.

On the other side of town, a 60-room property running Operra is having the same outage. The reception screen shows a calm
Offlinepill. The receptionist checks in three guests, charges a room-service order, swaps a room, and moves on. Nothing about the workflow has changed.

When the fibre comes back at 03:17 AM, every action quietly reconciles. The night audit closes the books on time. Nobody writes on paper. Nobody loses revenue. Nobody calls IT.

WHAT IT GIVES YOU

Continuity, not heroics.
Zero check-in delays

The front desk never blocks on a network call. Every action runs against a local truth.

Reconciles on its own

When the connection returns, every queued action syncs in the right order — no staff intervention.

Tamper-resistant by design

Hardware-clock verification stops anyone from gaming the system while disconnected.

30-day standalone window

Operate continuously through extended outages — and still close your books at month-end.

Per-property resilience

Each property's offline state is independent. One outage at one site doesn't affect any other.

Honest status, always

Staff always know whether they're online. The pill in the corner doesn't lie. No silent failures.
