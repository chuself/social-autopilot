THE STORY

Built for the moments owners actually care about.

End-of-day, end-of-month, end-of-quarter, surprise audit, board meeting, refinancing — the same four questions get asked every time: how much did we earn, how much did we collect, where is the gap, and who did what. Reports Hub answers all four in under 30 seconds.

Every number is computed live from the same ledger the front desk, restaurant and events team are posting into right now — no nightly batch, no “as of yesterday”, no exports stitched together in Excel.

And because Intelligence Hub sits on top, you also get cross-module revenue mix, peak-day spikes, revenue reconciliation drift and leakage signals — the things spreadsheets can never show you in time to act.
