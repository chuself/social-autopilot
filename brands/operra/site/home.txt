Operra HMS — Hotel & Restaurant Management Software for Tanzania & East Africa

Operra HMS is the hotel and restaurant management system built for Tanzanian hospitality. Offline-first PMS, TRA EFD VAT receipts, M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa and Airtel Money settlement, restaurant POS, housekeeping, events and corporate accounts — used by hotels in Dar es Salaam, Arusha, Mwanza, Zanzibar, Dodoma, Moshi, Iringa, Mbeya and across East Africa including Kenya, Uganda, Rwanda and Burundi.
