# Operra content pillars

Rotate in order. The brain must not repeat an angle used in the last 60 posts.

1. **Feature spotlight** — one concrete thing Operra does (front desk, housekeeping, POS,
   pricing, reports, audit trail). Show the outcome, not the button.
2. **Hotelier pain point** — name a daily frustration (lost bookings, cash leakage,
   double-booked rooms, night audit at 2am) and land Operra as the relief.
3. **Number that lands** — one credible stat about hotel operations or revenue leakage.
   Never invent figures; use only numbers from `facts.md`.
4. **Client proof** — a short, specific result from a real property. Approved quotes only.
5. **Swahili tip** — a genuinely useful operations tip in Kiswahili for Tanzanian hoteliers.
   Native register, not translated English.
6. **Industry note** — a real trend in East African hospitality with a practical takeaway.

## Voice
Direct, confident, quietly technical. Short sentences. No hype words
("revolutionary", "game-changer", "unlock"). No emoji spam — at most one, and only
where it genuinely helps scanning. Never promise what the product does not do.
